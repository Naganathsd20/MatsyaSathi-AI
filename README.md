# 🌊 MatsyaSathi AI

### Safer Seas. Smarter Fishing. Better Livelihoods.

**MatsyaSathi AI** is an AI-powered coastal fishing decision-support platform designed to help small-scale and traditional fishermen make safer and smarter decisions at sea.

The platform brings together **weather and sea-condition intelligence, fishing-zone recommendations, catch potential estimation, maritime safety alerts, market information, vessel planning, multilingual support, and an AI assistant** into a single easy-to-use interface.

> **Hackathon Theme:** AI for Public Good — Inclusive AI, Social Impact & Empowerment of Underserved Communities  
> **Target Community:** Small-scale, traditional and coastal fishermen in India  
> **Project Type:** Functional Web Prototype

---

## 🌊 Problem

Small-scale and traditional fishermen often make important decisions with limited access to timely and reliable information.

Before going to sea, a fisherman may need to answer questions such as:

- Is the sea safe today?
- What are the current weather and wave conditions?
- When is the best time to go fishing?
- Which fishing zone has better potential?
- What kind of catch can be expected?
- Are there any safety warnings?
- What are the current fish market prices?
- Which harbor and route should be considered?
- Where can reliable fishing-related information be accessed?

Information is often scattered across different sources and may be difficult to understand, especially for communities with limited digital access or limited technical literacy.

The challenge is therefore not only to provide more information, but to make useful information **simple, accessible, localized and actionable**.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

MatsyaSathi AI acts as a **digital fishing companion** for fishermen.

Instead of requiring users to search through multiple sources, the platform presents important fishing information in a unified dashboard.

The system focuses on three major outcomes:

### 🛡️ Safety

Helping fishermen understand sea conditions, weather risks and safety alerts before departure.

### 🎣 Better Fishing Decisions

Helping fishermen identify suitable fishing windows and potentially productive fishing zones.

### 💰 Better Livelihood Decisions

Providing catch estimates and market information to support better economic decisions.

# 🎯 Key Features

## 🌦️ Weather & Sea Intelligence

Provides a consolidated view of:

- Temperature
- Wind speed
- Wind direction
- Rain probability
- Visibility
- Wave height
- Sea condition
- Fishing conditions
- Recommended fishing window
- Short-term weather trends

The goal is to make complex environmental information easier to understand before a fishing trip.

---

## 🎣 Fishing Zone Recommendations

MatsyaSathi AI provides fishing-zone recommendations based on available sea-condition and zone information.

Users can view:

- Recommended zones
- Zone suitability
- Distance
- Sea condition
- Catch potential
- Depth
- Zone status
- Detailed zone information

Example statuses include:

- ⭐ Recommended
- ⚠️ Caution
- ❌ Avoid

This helps fishermen compare potential fishing locations before departure.

---

## 📈 Catch Potential Prediction

The platform provides an estimated catch-yield range for a planned fishing trip.

The prototype considers factors such as:

- Fishing zone
- Sea conditions
- Depth
- Environmental conditions
- Available fishing information

The dashboard presents the result in an easy-to-understand format such as an estimated kilogram range.

> Note: Catch values in the current prototype are demonstration estimates and should not be interpreted as guaranteed real-world catch predictions.

Official emergency and maritime safety resources remain the authoritative source for real-world emergencies.

# 🌐 Multilingual Accessibility

MatsyaSathi AI supports three languages:

- 🇬🇧 English
- 🇮🇳 ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada)
- 🇮🇳 हिन्दी (Hindi)

Users can change the application language from the Settings section.

The selected language is persisted so that the preference remains available across navigation and page refreshes.

This is an important part of the platform because language accessibility can reduce barriers for users who are more comfortable using regional languages.

# 🧠 AI & Intelligence Approach

The current submission is a **functional prototype**.

The application demonstrates how AI-assisted decision support can be integrated into a fisherman-focused platform.

The prototype currently uses structured application data and decision-support logic to demonstrate the complete user experience.

Future versions can integrate live:

- Ocean-condition APIs
- Weather APIs
- INCOIS data
- Market feeds
- Fishing datasets
- Machine-learning prediction models
- Location/GPS services
- Government and maritime information services

This approach allows the prototype to demonstrate the product workflow while leaving room for scalable real-world data integration.
